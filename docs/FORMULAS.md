# Simulation engineering parameters

Version 0.8. These are inspectable implementation details for the fictional compact league, **not a replacement GDD or approval of new full-world rules**. Approved catalogue, potential bands and striker weights follow living GDD revision 0.3 chapters 9–11 and 17. Other weights, rates and league settings are provisional calibration data stored in each career's configuration.

## Ability and knowledge

All 37 football attributes have internal decimal precision, bounded 1–100. `goalkeeping` is a retained legacy compatibility field, not a 38th displayed attribute. Overall is the rounded position-weighted mean; condition and morale do not alter this stored ability summary.

| Forward, approved striker baseline | Finishing .20, off the ball .15, composure .10, anticipation .10, acceleration .10, first touch .10, heading .08, pace .07, strength .05, decisions .05 |
| Defender, provisional | Tackling .20, marking .18, positioning .15, anticipation .10, heading .10, strength .08, concentration .07, pace .05, passing .04, decisions .03 |
| Midfielder, provisional | Passing .22, vision .15, decisions .13, first touch .12, technique .10, teamwork .08, stamina .07, positioning .05, tackling .04, work rate .04 |
| Goalkeeper, provisional | Reflexes .23, handling .20, one on ones .15, aerial command .12, positioning .10, communication .07, distribution .05, agility .05, rushing out .03 |

Initial coded potential samples uniformly once: −6 = 60–75, −7 = 70–85, −8 = 80–90, −9 = 85–95, −10 = 90–99. Generated values below current overall are corrected to overall; validation rejects subsequently invalid states. The fictional starter database uses −7 for youth, −6 for under-24 seniors and a small age-limited headroom above current overall for older seniors. Sampling frequencies and mature-player policy remain provisional. Reloads, reports and plan changes cannot reroll potential.

A report stores a dated noisy estimate with bounded ranges. Potential uncertainty is wider for youth. Monthly aging widens stored evidence using public age and elapsed time; it never refreshes against hidden truth. The own-club coaching report refreshes every 28 days. Old four-attribute reports remain incomplete until a new assessment. Full observer competence, evidence correlation, regional knowledge and discovery remain open.

## Development and recovery

Weekly growth uses base .30 × age factor × remaining headroom × training load × professionalism × minutes exposure × coaching facility factor. Age factor is clamped (29 − age)/10 in [0, 1.4], headroom is clamped (potential − overall)/20 in [0, 1], minutes exposure is .65 + .35 × min(1, weekly minutes/90), and facility factor is 1 + .06 × training level. Light/normal/intense loads multiply by .6/1/1.25. Growth follows the selected attribute group or all groups for Balanced. Intense load adds fatigue; injured players do not gain training growth. Physical decline starts above age 30 at .012 × (age − 30) per week.

Daily condition recovery is 4 + .05 × natural fitness, with +2 for light load or −2 for intense load; injury reduces it to 45%. Fatigue recovers separately. Medical records expose estimated dates and rehabilitation/conditioning stages, not a guaranteed true recovery date. External-player fitness/medical records are not exposed without assessment.

Quarterly reviews need at least ten weekly observations, 450 minutes, measured development and sufficient professionalism. Under-24 positive revisions are capped at one per quarter and three in a rolling year. Current implementation makes no upward revisions at 24+; exceptional mature development, negative setback reviews, positional retraining and full staff capability effects remain unfinished. Hidden adaptability, consistency and ambition are stored for further work; their full approved behavioural effects are not claimed implemented.

## Match actions and competition

The engine stores a separate RNG stream, elapsed minutes, phase clock, possession zone, active players, bench, events and action statistics. Each minute resolves a representative possession action, not every real-world pass. Passing attempts use a bounded logistic comparison of relevant attributes. Effective attributes include condition, fatigue, sharpness and morale. Home advantage, player numbers, manager skill and tactical risk modify action execution separately from database ability.

Shot quality starts in .03–.21 for ordinary attempts, .05–.17 for crosses, and .76 for penalties, then adjusts for movement, positioning and risk. Goal probability adjusts shot-quality log odds by finishing versus goalkeeping. xG is the pre-finishing chance estimate, not a calibrated claim against real football data. Ratings reflect recorded passes, tackles, saves, goals, errors and fouls; victory itself grants no rating bonus.

Default representative-action rates: foul .18; caution given foul .19; straight red given foul .003; injury .003 before fatigue adjustment; penalty given final-third foul .045. Match condition cost is .19 × (1.25 − stamina/200). Manager changes respond to low condition and score state. Added time is 1–3 minutes in the first half and 2–5 in the second. Extra-time halves add one minute each. Shootout kicks are separate from match goals and bonus events.

Northshire rules: 25 senior places, under-21 exemption from that count, minimum age 16, at most 17 non-homegrown seniors, five incoming loans, eleven starters, nine bench places, five changes in three in-play windows. Half-time changes do not use a window. Five accumulated yellows cause a one-match ban; second yellow and direct red use one and three matches respectively. Loans cannot play against their parent. Employment and registration remain separate; list omission does not stop wages. Minimum seven available players; a single failure awards 0–3, and a double pre-match failure records 0–0. Abandonment preserves real goals and adds administrative goals to award the opponent at least a three-goal winning margin. These are configurable fictional league settings, not claims about any real competition's current regulations.

Remaining football scope includes richer formations/roles, complete restart and set-piece sequences, goalkeeper dismissal decisions, nuanced tactical explanations, workload/injury susceptibility calibration, suspension appeals and competition-specific discipline resets. Extra-time/shootout logic is tested but not yet reachable through a generated cup competition.

## Staff, authority and rival clubs (0.8)

The nine departmental roles use ten capability fields from the approved staff model. The compact database contains 27 free candidates and three opening staff per rival club. Initial references use ±12-point ranges, interviews ±6, and own working observations ±3 around a stable noisy estimate. These are implementation tuning, not a claim of precise assessment. The original three-manager hiring desk remains separate.

Owner recruitment takes one day before interview, then two days to join from unemployment or seven days from another club. Employer compensation is four weeks of the old salary, transferred at binding acceptance. New salary is reserved immediately and enters actual payroll on the joining date. Contracts last one to three compact seasons. Notice is the lesser of four weeks' salary and remaining guaranteed salary; paid compensation is not refunded if the new appointment is later cancelled. Forecasts include joining and expiry dates. Actual staff salary is included in each club's existing payroll/accrual model.

Each responsibility consumes 25 of 100 capacity units, permitting at most four per employee. Fully allocated owner staff gain two workload points per week; otherwise workload falls by three, bounded 0–100. Relevant departmental capability is reduced by max(.7, 1 − workload/400). Training growth multiplies by 1 + (coaching capability − 50)/250. Scouting report radius multiplies by 1 − (assessment capability − 50)/150, rounded with a minimum radius of four. A medical lead adds operations capability/100 to daily condition recovery. Other capability effects, richer availability and personality behaviours remain incomplete.

Default delegated authority is £80,000 per department and £300,000 club-wide over a rolling 28 days, with a 365-day duration ceiling. Active conditional reservations remain counted until completed or released, even when older than the rolling period. Employment exposure includes signing/transfer fees and guaranteed wages, less remaining existing base wages for renewals. Academy admission includes the fee and guaranteed salary. Project approval includes construction cost and 365 days of upkeep. Performance bonuses/options cannot be auto-authorised. Owner approval can grant a one-off authority exception but cannot bypass domain affordability, consent, medical or registration checks. Cases expire after seven days. These defaults are configurable saved tuning, not new GDD financial policy.

AI clubs review every seven days. Their wage ceiling is 90% of configured weekly operating income minus configured overheads. Cash cover is three weeks of existing payroll/overheads plus pending wages, alongside all scheduled transfer bills and reserved fees. Appointments and renewals check these same commitments; owner-originated outgoing player deals also enforce the receiving club's limits. Target/max senior squad sizes are 19/20. Player selection uses persisted noisy overall estimates, public role, salary and fee; observation uncertainty depends on assessment staff and refreshes after 28 days. Conditional AI registrations take two days and have an 8% seeded medical-withdrawal chance. Final completion rechecks eligibility and financial commitments, transfers the actual fee, settles sell-on rights, pays signing costs and changes employment/registration. No extra funds are injected to make a transfer succeed.

Rival finances still use the provisional aggregate weekly income introduced in 0.5. Full supporter/commercial economics, complex AI negotiations, manager/staff parity and financially complete long-term population policy are future work. The compact implementation is not the full-world economic or 50-season release gate.


## Domestic cup milestone 0.10

GDD chapters 3, 7, 17, 26–27 and QA02–04/10 guide this implementation. Development data in `world.json.cup` reserves season-relative days 22, 50 and 78 with minimum three-day rest from league dates. These compact-season dates are provisional and do not replace the approved August–May world calendar. Draws use an isolated seed keyed by competition, season and round; sorting entrant IDs first makes JSON ordering irrelevant. Each round is drawn only after its predecessor settles.

For N entrants, opening byes = 2^ceil(log2(N)) - N. All other clubs play once. Winners plus byes enter the next round; the engine handles regulation, extra time and shootouts. Shootout kicks never count as match goals. If both clubs cannot field seven players, an explicit seeded administrative draw advances one at 0–0 without played appearances or bonuses; single failures use the inherited 3–0 award. No automatic replacement players are created for a tie.

Cup results do not post league points, goal difference or league form. Player seasonal totals currently aggregate league and cup; per-competition player breakdowns remain future work. Registration lists, loan-parent restrictions and disciplinary accumulators are shared domestic rules at this stage. There is no cup-tied eligibility rule in this provisional database. Owner home gates use the existing ticket/usable-capacity calculation; rival finances retain their aggregate-income limitation. The configured winner prize is zero, so trophy completion creates no invented payment. Non-zero configured prizes use the winning entity's idempotent ledger posting.

Schema 8 adds the competition graph. Schema 1–7 migration leaves the current season's fixtures and RNG unchanged; the first cup is created on the next rollover. Season history retains the graph and trophy; unresolved future cup rounds prevent season completion. Promotion/relegation, multiple leagues and full calendar solving remain outstanding.

## Update 0.19 starting selection and shape

Eligibility uses the existing registration/medical/discipline rules before any scoring. Readiness score = position-weighted current ability × (.45 + .55 × condition / 100) − fatigue × .12. Fresh legs additionally subtracts fatigue × .15. Continuity adds 2 for starting the most recent completed current-season fixture within 14 days. Develop prospects adds up to 3 × youth-development / 100 for players aged at most 21 whose current ability is within 5 points of the best eligible player in their own broad position. No potential, reputation or wage term enters selection. These coefficients are configurable provisional tuning, capped by validation.

Rotation choices (Balanced, Fresh legs, Continuity) and youth choices (Readiness first, Develop prospects) persist from an independent manager-selection identity stream. New preferred shapes are Balanced 4-4-2, Attacking 4-3-3 and Cautious 4-5-1. Migration does not rewrite any existing preferred shape, capability, risk instruction, assessment or active match.

If short, tactical judgement at least the configured 40 and Adaptability at least 50 permit choosing the shape with the fewest missing natural-position places, only when strictly better. Ties prefer the original shape then the definition order. This represents broad starting personnel shape, not detailed positional roles or tactical familiarity. Missing places use eligible outfield cover before extra goalkeepers; the report displays actual natural-position counts and warns when fewer than eleven players or no goalkeeper are available.

Within each position, score descending then player ID selects starters. Remaining bench choices retain the existing goalkeeper-first coverage rule, then selection score and ID. ## Tactical preparation — Update 0.20

Separate per-player records key learning by formation plus manager style. New and migrated records start absent (Not assessed); unrecorded preparation supplies zero added edge, not a penalty. No past training is reconstructed. Appointments record a dated brief without resetting records, modifying attributes or granting preparation. Memory is retained without decay in this first pathway.

Once per advancing day, after medical/recovery processing, the owned senior squad trains unless there is an owned-club fixture that day. Retired/youth players are excluded; injured players, condition below 65 or fatigue above 70 miss the session. No learning is backfilled for missed days or newly joined players. A return to an earlier system retains its prior record.

Daily gain = 0.8 × (0.5 + teaching) × (1 + .04 × min(5, training facility level)) × load × condition/100 × (1 − fatigue/100) × (1 − preparation/100). Teaching = (manager Coaching + workload-adjusted active departmental Coaching)/200; no departmental employee contributes zero, leaving the manager to teach. Existing staff workload adjusts the departmental capability. Light load uses .5; normal and intense use 1, while their existing recovery/fatigue effects remain. Gains are capped at 100, rounded to six decimals. No attribute or potential bonus is added.

New owned-club match snapshots record every starter and bench player's preparation in the actual selected formation/style. The passing-action edge is the mean of current on-pitch recorded values /100 × 2, subtracting the opposing side's equivalent once. This is recomputed after substitutions/dismissals and is not applied again to finishing, selection, morale or a squad dashboard total. Missing histories and the unchanged AI training baseline contribute zero. Snapshots and maximum effect are frozen for save/resume; public reports contain only owned-player learned records. Old matches without a snapshot receive zero additional edge and no retrospective learning.

Once a fixture is settled, actual participants receive 1.2 × min(90, played minutes)/90 × (1 − preparation/100) in its snapshotted system. Unused substitutes and forfeits earn nothing. Extra-time exposure is capped; shootouts add no training minutes. Fixture settlement guards prevent duplicate credit. Configuration stores daily gain, match gain, maximum edge and light-load factor; bounds are validated. All coefficients and the asymmetric owned-club pathway are provisional, not a calibrated claim about football. Pairwise cohesion, relationship evidence, alternative training schedules and wider AI training remain pending.


## Contracts and loans — Update 0.21

New employment defaults are zero amounts/rates and no player option. Amounts are integer pence; annual rises are 0–25%, relegation cuts 0–50%, release amounts at most £1m and promotion bonuses at most £100,000 in this development ruleset. The allowed-employment list gates offered fields. It is configurable plumbing, not a verified national legal catalogue. Annual salary multiplies by (100 + rate)/100 with integer flooring on each actual signing anniversary, using leap-year-safe dates. Forecasts use the same dated escalation. Relegation cuts apply once at division rollover. Promotion bonus is earned once per employment agreement.

A player option is considered within 14 days of expiry. The first deterministic policy accepts security if morale is at least 40 or current weekly wage meets the existing domestic benchmark (450 + current overall × 13) pounds; otherwise it declines. This is provisional player behaviour, not the complete reputation/interest model. Club and player options cannot coexist. A player option is an existing commitment, so its exercise does not require discretionary chairman approval.

A release amount caps the normal quote and can override the seller's minimum-squad veto when paid in full. It never bypasses personal consent, medical or registration. Transfer appearance and promotion payments are single fixed amounts, expiring at the new employment end; appearance counts use actual participants and fixture IDs. Promotion refers to the buying club's first promotion during that period. Earned amounts settle against available counterparty cash, retain unpaid arrears and survive employment exit. No unearned contingent payment is inserted into the guaranteed forecast.

An outgoing sale's possible conditional payments reduce the guaranteed quote pound for pound after the existing sell-on discount. The possible total cannot exceed that valuation. This conservative policy prevents free, unpriced bonus claims; it is not a completed probability-based AI model. Incoming club offers still must meet the existing minimum fixed fee.

Loan purchase fees freeze the current transfer quote when selected; permanent wages freeze the player's then-current wage, and employment runs for two scenario seasons from agreement. Optional purchases reserve no capacity before exercise. Appearance-conditioned obligations reserve the full possible purchase fee and additional permanent wage capacity during acceptance and the active loan, irrespective of whether the appearance condition has yet been met. Recall is forbidden for binding purchases. Actual match participation counts once; unused substitutes and forfeits do not.

At loan end + 1, a met obligation settles; otherwise the loan returns. The first implementation only accepts obligations whose settlement fits the current registration window. Optional purchase by an AI borrower is considered on the final loan day if its recorded appearance threshold and cash, bill-cover, wage and registration checks pass. The borrower wage forecast replaces the temporary share with the permanent wage after a triggered binding purchase. Fees, original employment closure, loan status, payroll responsibility and registration effects settle atomically.


### Dated transfer rights and player buy-outs

Schema 20 adds empty rights, notices and buy-out receipts to schema 19, plus configurable mechanism profiles. Existing employment and match state are unchanged. Northshire and Spain profiles support transfer releases and buy-outs; other profiles default to transfer releases. These are development mechanism gates, not verified national-law parity.

Buy-backs start at the next scenario season and expire at the end of one to three future seasons. The second price starts with the second future season. Each exercise also requires the current registration window, full gross payment, fresh employment consent and medical/registration checks. Renewal preserves surviving club rights; release or the next permanent departure ends them. Existing sell-on is settled separately on a buy-back transfer. A player buy-out instead pays recorded compensation and closes transfer-only rights without triggering their sell-on or first-refusal conditions.

First-refusal notices freeze the complete package, including deferred/conditional fees, sell-on and retained rights. The response deadline is the earliest of three configured days, right expiry, or the offer/window expiry minus medical days and one day for timeout recovery. Exact-package decline/timeout is not re-notified; changed terms require a new notice. Matching freezes club terms but requires separate player employment. Underlying sale cancellation/expiry invalidates dependent matched consent and releases its reservations. Failed matching resumes an original offer only while its deadlines remain valid.

Initial retained-right discounts are 20% for buy-back and 5% for first refusal, combined with the previous sell-on discount before conditional amounts replace guaranteed proceeds. This is conservative provisional valuation, not risk pricing. AI evaluates matching packages against normal asking value, total cash commitments, payroll and registration. Its new-exit offer is a 10% wage rise and extended security; a player with morale at least 85 requires 20%, otherwise 5%, and an employment end beyond the current contract. This deliberately simple consent policy remains separate from the approved full reputation/interest system. AI matched purchases reserve the full fee, signing fee and possible transfer add-ons. New AI employment in enabled profiles sets a buy-out at twice the normal asking fee, capped at the development limit of £1m; no such terms are invented on migration.

Pre-agreed loan purchase terms are rejected when an active first-refusal right belongs to a third club; a normal loan remains available. This prevents the loan route bypassing the required notification.

## Update 0.23 reputation progression (policy 1; provisional calibration)

All coefficients live in `config.reputation`. Public standing stays integer 1–100. Review intervals are 28 days from career creation/upgrade; a missed interval advances its due timestamp without inventing backdated events. Individual reviews require at least two played matches and 90 real minutes within the window. Club reviews normally require two matches; season closure flushes a shorter final window. Forfeits, abandonments and legacy-engine matches without reliable statistics are excluded from performance evidence. No training, CA, potential, transfer fee, salary or match RNG is read.

Player target = minute-weighted prior-period competition standing + (minute-weighted public match rating − 6.5) × 5. Review movement = clamp((target − prior-period player standing) × .06, −1, +1). Club target = mean prior-period competition standing + (mean result score − .5) × 12, where win/draw/loss score 1/.5/0. Club movement = clamp((target − prior-period club standing) × .08, −1, +1). An extra-time draw decided on penalties stays a draw for this performance component; the trophy is credited separately.

Season closure: league and cup winners gain +1 club standing; recorded contributors with at least 90 minutes for that club/competition gain +1 player standing. Promotion adds +1 club standing; relegation subtracts 1. Competition movement = clamp((mean prior-period participant-club standing − prior-period competition standing) × .08, −1, +1). All linked references are frozen before the award batch; league records are reused rather than duplicated as cup records.

Fractional movement carries between reviews and integer changes truncate toward zero. Reaching an index boundary or season cap discards blocked carry. Total absolute changes across every performance/award event are capped per season: players 5, clubs 6, competitions 2. Opposite movements consume the same budget. Carry and budgets reset at season rollover; reputation, baselines and all dated reviews persist. No-evidence players retain stature without an automatic inactivity penalty. Pending minutes and honour contributions retain the original club/competition association through transfers. Duplicate fixture IDs and season awards are rejected by persisted receipts.

Schema 22 adds reputation evidence, reference snapshots, review deadlines and domestic-cup records. Previous schemas retain public values, agreements, cash and in-progress match state; already completed fixtures and cup honours already settled at migration are outside new evidence coverage. No future performance is inferred from reputation. Changes flow into the existing recruitment policy at its next consent assessment, retaining grandfathered pre-policy consent rules. Match-rating quality and all coefficients require wider balancing; this implementation is not a statistically calibrated reputation model.


## Update 0.24 playing-time agreements (provisional calibration)

Working agreements are distinct from wage contracts, assessed squad importance and reputation. All new-policy settings live in `config.playing_time`; each signed agreement freezes its target, review interval, minimum fixture count and meaningful-start threshold. Default review: 28 days, at least 3 available senior domestic league/cup fixtures, meaningful start requires 30 actual minutes. Key starter targets 75% meaningful starts, regular starter 55%; rotation targets 30% of available 90-minute opportunities, squad cover 10%, development prospect 15%. Development prospect is a minutes role, not a bespoke development plan. Extra time is capped at 90 minutes per fixture for this review.

Kickoff snapshots record availability and starting status for every covered player, including omitted players. Injury, suspension, medical fitness and other eligibility exceptions excuse a fixture. Non-registration is an unexcused club omission. An in-match injury excuses the whole fixture in this first policy. Forfeits, abandonments and old active matches without snapshots are outside coverage. Evidence IDs prevent duplicate settlement. Review windows are [start, due); catch-up partitions each window separately. Insufficient available fixtures give no verdict and do not erase an older concern.

A below-agreement review creates one persistent private concern ID. It deducts 6 recruitment utility points at the responsible club until a fulfilled review; no daily stacking, direct morale change, teammate reaction or ability adjustment. An explicit credible offered role adds up to 6 × private minutes priority / 100 × target / 100 utility, with no credit while an unresolved concern exists. Capacity sums recorded position targets and pending incoming commitments against 100 × broad positional slots (1/4/4/2); a conflict removes the bonus and records a warning but is not a selection order. This is a provisional capacity check, not a full manager endorsement or tactical compatibility forecast. AI new arrivals offer Rotation only where this check allows it; older pending AI deals retain their original lack of promises.

A role conversation is repeatable only after one configured review period. Existing roles may change on a completed review day, preserving evidence, the next deadline and unresolved concerns. A reduction is accepted only with an existing concern and private minutes priority at most 60; otherwise the refusal is recorded. Renewals retain the original role and evidence. Open employment talks must finish or be withdrawn before a separate role change. Permanent departures archive the agreement; parent-club agreements survive temporary loans with coverage suspended. Staff dismissal, division changes and CA/reputation changes do not rewrite terms.

Schema 23 adds empty agreement/history/conversation registers and settings to earlier saves. No past promise or missed opportunity is invented. Live match inputs remain private; the owner sees only their club agreements and their historical records. This first milestone does not implement relationship propagation, influence, custom competition scopes, staged progression targets, manager endorsement or the full circumstance-based morale model.

## Update 0.25 morale sources

Morale = clamp(50 + sum(capped group contributions), 1, 100). Playing-time shortfall = -(8 + 8 × existing ambition / 100), group cap ±20. Source identity includes onset; it persists until fulfilment/closure and suspends away from the responsible club. Result expectation = clamp(0.5 + pre-match public club reputation gap / 100, .15, .85). Participants with minutes receive 8 × (actual - expectation), actual = 1/.5/0 for win/draw/loss. Result group cap ±10; linear seven-day decay. Administrative/unsnapshotted matches excluded; shootouts use the played score. Imported mood transitions linearly over fourteen days without invented historical causes.

Bands: 1–20 Very unhappy; >20–40 Unhappy; >40–60 Content; >60–80 Happy; >80–100 Delighted. Seven-day trend threshold ±3. First-team mean unweighted; injured/suspended included, academy/departed/loaned-out excluded. Trend uses shared cohort, with membership changes separately reported. No summary multiplier. Coefficients remain provisional.

## Update 0.26 private relationship follow-up

A first meaningful contact creates a neutral directed player-to-current-club-chairman link (trust/respect/alignment 50). One unresolved playing-time breach reduces trust by 6 and respect by 2; subsequent below-target windows do not repeat that penalty. A fulfilled recorded window increases trust by 2 and respect by 1, bounded 0–100, and clears the active grievance. Closure marks the grievance no longer active; its marker decays after 28 days but history/trust remain. Alignment is reserved state and gains no invented evidence. No extra relationship match multiplier.

Support requires fourteen days since the last meeting and a new topic. An unresolved concern has one topic per onset. General wellbeing topics use fourteen-day buckets, with the cooldown separately enforced. Trust at least 35 is required for reassurance. A player with an ongoing concern must be listened to and have existing ambition ≤65 to receive one temporary morale point; an unconcerned receptive player receives three. Other responses give zero. Support shares a ±5 group cap and linear seven-day decay. It neither changes trust nor clears an agreement. Existing hidden traits and exact scores are not exposed. Coefficients remain provisional, not real-world calibrated constants.

## Club dynamics 0.27

GDD 16/17/34, provisional coefficients in `config.dynamics`. No changes to attributes, CA, wages, role promises or the morale formula. Existing players start as established members with **no inferred social links**. New first-team arrivals (including transfers, returns and youth promotions) begin a fresh settling record; historical club-specific links remain attached to their original club.

A completed non-fixture day records shared contact for senior teammates who are out of rehabilitation, at condition >=65 and fatigue <=70. Directed familiarity increases by `training_gain * (.5 + .5 * min(1, (daysA+1)/28, (daysB+1)/28))`, capped at 100. The default gain is 1. This is social contact, not extra tactical practice or attribute growth. A newcomer settles after `28 * (1.5 - hidden_adaptability/100)` attended days. That trait remains hidden. Fixture days record actual concurrent on-pitch simulation minutes, including extra time, at .025 familiarity per minute. Unused substitutes, shootouts, forfeits, abandoned games and legacy matches without snapshots supply no social match evidence. Each fixture settles once. Membership reconciliation uses stable player-ID order, including after save/load; distinct same-day departures keep distinct event IDs.

Working conduct adjusts directed trust by `.08 * (counterpart_professionalism-50)/50 * min(1, familiarity_gain)` and respect by half that amount, each clamped 0–100. This is a provisional conduct inference from completed shared activity, not an objective personality disclosure. Alignment is retained at neutral until an applicable event supplies evidence. Acquaintance alone does not establish high trust.

Influence within a selected group is the mean incoming `min(1,familiarity/50) * max(0,(trust-50)/50)` across its other members. Missing links contribute zero. CA, wages, reputation, age and formal captaincy do not supply influence. For each directed pair, cooperation is `clamp((.7*familiarity/100 + .3*(trust-50)/50) * (1+.2*target_influence), -1, 1)`. Missing pairs contribute zero. The dashboard is `50+50*mean(pair_cooperation)`; no observed pairs means Not assessed, not a guessed score. Coverage counts directed player relationships. Seven-day trend requires the identical cohort and dated evidence; a >=3-point change is improving/declining, otherwise stable. A changed cohort is Not comparable.

Passing actions add the acting player's mean pair cooperation with current on-pitch teammates times 1.5, minus the defender's equivalent. Each side is bounded to ±1.5 ability-scale points. Substitutions/dismissals change the contributing membership. This is the sole social coordination term: the dashboard score and hierarchy are not added again. Inputs freeze at kickoff and survive save/load. Preparation and morale keep their existing distinct effects. Unplayed old active matches retain their original path. All active clubs use the same contact, settling and coordination rules.

Bench requests record the manager and available employed Coaching staff as witnesses, with football agreement distinct from process reaction. They do not make the conversation public or inform players/other departments. An attacking request earns supporting football feedback when behind or from an Ambitious coach; otherwise it may be questioned. A manager or coach outside an Advisory remit may object to pressure even while supporting the football intent. The existing bench response/trust mechanism is retained; new staff reaction records do not add another performance multiplier. A first unresolved pressure episode reduces the recorded directed trust by 3 and respect by 2. Repeated pressure refreshes its evidence without stacking the penalty. Private Listen/Respect remit follow-ups are once per episode; words alone confer no trust. A subsequent backing message after Respect remit repairs 2 trust/1 respect and closes that minor concern. Otherwise the active concern ages out after 28 days but history and trust remain. Victory itself performs no repair.

Limits: this provides a working senior first-team contact network, current-group influence and witnessed bench reactions. It does not claim completed leak/faction/mentoring events, full staff personality and departmental cooperation, staff promises/resources, or succession identities. The one-message immediate bench interaction remains the inherited limited interface; delayed multi-strength intervention resolution remains open in the full GDD. Tuning and realism calibration need playtesting.
